# Code of Conduct

AirBar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful. Disagree about ideas, not people. Harassment, insults, discrimination and publishing others' private information are not acceptable anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions or other spaces.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer ([@Subham12R](https://github.com/Subham12R)) privately. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content, or warn or ban participants, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
