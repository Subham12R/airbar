# Getting help

1. **Check the [troubleshooting table](README.md#troubleshooting)** in the README. It covers sign-in, empty homes, busy units and Gatekeeper.
2. **Search [existing issues](https://github.com/Subham12R/airbar/issues?q=is%3Aissue)**. Someone may have hit the same thing.
3. **Ask a question or report a bug** with a [new issue](https://github.com/Subham12R/airbar/issues/new/choose). Please include:
   - macOS version and AirBar version (Settings → About)
   - your AC model, if known
   - what you did, what you expected, and what happened, with any error message
   - for unit-specific problems, the output of `airbar status --json`, with names or IDs removed if you prefer

AirBar is a volunteer project and isn't supported by Hitachi. For problems with the AC itself, the Wi-Fi adapter or the airCloud Go app, contact Hitachi support.
